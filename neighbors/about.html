Why Neighbors

The right apartment is often one filter away.

Rental sites only show you what passes every checkbox. Neighbors ranks every NYC rental by how close it is to what you want, so the near-misses you'd happily take stay on the list.

The problem

Filters say yes or no. Your preferences don't.

A $3,550 apartment isn't wrong for a $3,500 budget. It's $50 off. Here's the same search two ways.

What you get

A search that thinks like you do

Every listing gets a 0–100 match score and one plain-language note about its biggest tradeoff, so you can decide fast.

Near-misses, ranked

Slightly over budget or one stop farther out? It still shows up, ordered by how close it is. You decide what's worth it.

Learns your taste

Like a few places and similar ones rise. After five likes it learns which features you really care about.

Verified as real

Each address is checked against NYC building records. Phantom or non-residential listings get flagged before you waste a visit.

Your real commute

Add work or a friend's place and every card shows the trip by subway or PATH, step by step, with a link to live directions.

Remembers the market

Every search adds to a running picture: what's new, what dropped in price, and a price history for each apartment.

Private by design

It runs on your computer. Your searches and likes stay here, and API keys live in your system keychain.

Built to be trusted

Honest by default

A ranking is only useful if you can trust it, so a few rules are built in.

Your limits always win

Likes and learning only reorder results. They never stretch your budget or your area.

Unknown isn't "no"

A listing that doesn't mention a dishwasher isn't excluded. Missing data is a small penalty, not a dealbreaker.

Estimates are labeled

Affordable rents and commute times are marked as estimates, and guessed amenities say "likely".

Clean data only

A licensed API and NYC open data, no scraping. Links take you to the source instead of copying it.

Gentle on free tiers

Responses are cached and requests rate-limited, and fetching stops at 45 of RentCast's 50 free monthly calls.

Nothing leaves your machine

The app only listens on your own computer. There's no account, no tracking, and no upload.
